CampusFest began in 2026 as a one-day hackathon in Block C. Seven years later it is a four-day festival with 16 sessions, 699 seats and a stage big enough for eight student bands. Every event is organised by students, free to attend and open to every year group and department.

Registration is intentionally simple: no account, no payment, no app. Pick an event, fill in six fields and your pass is ready. Organisers can then see the live student list and participation numbers in the dashboard.

Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Do I need to pay or create an account?

No. Every event is free for currently enrolled students and registration only needs your name, student ID, email, phone and year. There is no payment, login or app download.

Can I register for more than one event?

Yes — and you should. Each registration creates a separate pass, so register once per event. Use the “Register another” button on your pass to start the next one.

Where is my registration stored?

In your own browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server. That means your pass stays on this device and the organiser dashboard shows only registrations made in this browser.

What happens if an event is full?

Full events are marked and cannot be selected. Seats update as people register, so check back — a cancelled registration releases a seat immediately.

How do I know which event to pick?

Tap “Ask the fest guide” in the corner (or the link under the hero buttons). Tell it what you enjoy — coding, music, sport, careers, photography — and it will suggest an event, answer questions about times, venues, seats and what to bring, and pre-fill the registration form when you choose one.

How do I get a team for the hackathon or 3v3?

Register as an individual and add a note in the form, or email the student council. We match solo builders and players with teams in the week before the festival.
